Welcome to Puzzlelopolis

Puzzlelopolis is a monthly puzzle zine available for free at your nearest coffee shop / third place in Ann Arbor, Michigan. It is also a place where the bus always arrives on time and your holds are always available for pickup at the library. If you enjoy the zine, please tell a friend! We're always welcoming new folks to Puzzlelopolis.
If you would like to be notified when new issues of Puzzlelopolis are available, you can sign up for our newsletter below. Or you can directly go to any of our drop-off locations listed below to see if you can snag a copy. You can also always reach me at puzzlelopolis@gmail.com.

Print Back Issues

The archive of past Puzzlelopolis issues are available for you to print on your own. The Ann Arbor District Library offers 30 pages of free printing per day, so that's always a good option.
Use the following settings when printing:
  • Letter sized
  • Black & white
  • Double sided
  • Flip on the short edge
  • Don't scale to fit, use default size (the file already contains 1/4" margins on all sides)
