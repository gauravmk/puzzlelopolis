Step by step solution to Weekend at the Mills

This is one of my most challenging puzzles so far and it's not obvious how to solve it from just the solution. Let's walk through how you might solve this. Obvious spoilers ahead.
1. Let's start with Clue #1. It tells us a set of activities in each week. This means these activities are mutually exclusive, they can't go together. Let's cross out all combinations of these four items in the grid.
2. Clue #2 gives us a straightforward way of eliminating two squares. Clue #4 also gives a confirmed pair, so we can eliminate everything in that section that is in the same row/column of that confirmed pair (If pumpkin pie goes with empire apples, it can't go with any other kind of apple).
3. Going back to clue #3, this tells us two different things. Firstly, the maple taffy wasn't eaten at the petting farm, since they are connected to different apples. Secondly, if the maple taffy goes with honeycrisp or gala apples, that means it can't go with fuji or empire. Same reasoning holds for the petting farm. Let's eliminate all those possibilities.
4. All the clues are in, now time to make deductions from the grid. If empire and pumpkin pie are in the same week, and empire isn't with the petting farm, then the pumpkin pie can't be at the petting farm. Let's eliminate that.
5. That leaves only one option for which treat goes with the petting farm. It must be hot cider! Which means we can again cross out anything from that section in the same row or column.
6. Using the same logic as before, petting farm and cider go together, and cider doesn't go with honeycrisp. So petting farm + honeycrisp is a no-go. Eliminating that leaves only one apple for petting farm. Let's add that in, and X-out a few more squares as a result.
7. I do see that honeycrisp/hayride crossing, but before we get to it, I want to show one other type of logical deduction you can make. Petting farm goes with hot cider. And it also goes with gala apples. Therefore, hot cider and gala apples must go together. I'm checking that off and x-ing out the relevant squares as a result.
8. We now have 3 guaranteed squares (because 3 others in their row/column are x-ed out). Just going through and filling all of those in. This completes the Apple X Treat quadrant.
9. Cinnamon donuts go with fuji, but not with corn maze. So we can cross out fuji+maze. That is enough to solve the Apple x Activity quadrant.
10. Once you have two quadrants solve, you have all the information you need. Haunted House = Fuji = Cinnamon Donuts, so we can fill in Haunted House + Donuts on the last quadrant. Following similar logic for all the other combinations, you get a completely solved puzzle!
